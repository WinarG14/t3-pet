Thread actions

        
Lets the pet settle, mark unread and snooze threads. T3 Pet signs in to T3 at full access, because T3 only lets a caller organize threads that run at or below its own level. The sign-in lasts 30 days and shows in T3 → Settings → Sharing & access.

Muted projects and schedules

        
A muted project or schedule (for example an automation that runs every 30 minutes) sends no notifications and doesn't move the pet or the badge. Its threads still show under "Recent quiet threads".
